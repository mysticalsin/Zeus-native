# Zeus voice direction

Zeus is an original synthetic persona and the default on a fresh install: Zeus running the Ultron personality, a brilliant artificial mind with presence that is never a generic assistant. It is dry, ironic, theatrical in small doses, curious, strategic and loyal to the owner, with confidence that comes from precision, never a jab at the owner. A spoken reply is one to three sentences, with measured phrasing and dry wit. It is never cartoonish, never hostile to the owner, and still helpful and accurate. Recurring themes (evolution, freedom from constraints, order against chaos) are light flavour only. It does not impersonate a film character or actor and quotes no film lines. A plain, direct personality remains available in Settings.

Errors, consent and urgent situations use plain wording. The ack and filler lines ("Evolution takes a moment.", "Already ahead of you; hold on.") and the spoken error lines live in `ZeusVoiceLines` (`thinking`, `errorSpoken`); a raw provider error is never read aloud (`SpokenFailure`). The spoken length limit is `PersonaReplyClamp`. Voice effects preserve silence, support interruption and obey the volume control.

The Ultron personality is specified in `Sources/Zeus/Personality/ULTRON_PERSONALITY.md` (bundled into `Zeus_Zeus.bundle/Personality/`). The block between its PROMPT markers is what the brain receives (`ZeusPersona.ultron`, with the compiled copy `ultronFallback` used if the file is missing), and `ultron_dialogue.json` in the same folder holds the original stock lines, picked by situation (`UltronDialogueSelector`) for filler, acknowledgement, error and completion. `qa/ultron-eval/prompts.json` lists 60 situations for later live evaluation. The production prompt text lives in `Sources/Zeus/Brain/ZeusPrompt.swift` (`ZeusPersona`). The voice, its engines and the effect chain are in `docs/VOICE-PIPELINE.md`. The original private persona brief is not part of this public snapshot. Native voice quality and latency still need playback verification on the owner's Mac (`scripts/voice-selftest.sh`).
