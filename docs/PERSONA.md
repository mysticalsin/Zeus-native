# Zeus voice direction

Zeus offers a calm, precise synthetic persona and a plain, direct alternative. The synthetic delivery uses measured phrasing and occasional dry wit, without impersonating a film character or actor. Errors, consent and urgent situations use plain wording. Voice effects must preserve silence, support interruption and obey the volume control.

The production prompt is in `Sources/Zeus/Brain/ZeusPrompt.swift`. The original private persona brief is not part of this public snapshot. Native voice quality and latency still require automated corpus and playback verification.
